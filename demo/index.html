Photosensitivity note

Lithophane adds no flashes of its own: nothing in it moves with time. But a lamp behind a plate brightens a clip's dark parts, so a dark clip's own flicker can become a large flash. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.

At its defaults it goes over the line on one of Resolume's demo clips, NeonRoom2_32: in that clip's worst second, over a third of the picture, the brightness swings 4 times on its own and 7 times through the plugin, which is three and a half flashes a second (more than three is over the line). This page opens on the defaults over the harness's card, which does not move, and that view, measured the same way from the page's own output for 20 seconds, made no flash at all. The page's other clips, at the defaults for 20 seconds each, made none across the picture and at most half a flash a second in the worst third of it. A clip of your own is brightened the same way. Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
